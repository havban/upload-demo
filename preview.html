3 · Previewing the file without downloading it

PDFs are rendered a page at a time with pdf.js, so a 5,800-page document opens as fast as a one-page one. Workbooks are rendered into a virtualised grid: only the ~30 rows you can see exist in the DOM, and the rest are pulled from the parsing worker as you scroll — which is what keeps 250,000 rows scrolling smoothly.
