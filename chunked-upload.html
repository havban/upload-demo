1 · Chunked upload of a large file

Pick (or generate) a 50 MB PDF or Excel file. It is sliced into parts, each part is hashed and sent separately with several in flight at once, failures are retried with backoff, and the whole thing can be paused, resumed — even across a page reload — or cancelled. The bar below tracks bytes actually accepted by the server, not bytes handed to the network.
